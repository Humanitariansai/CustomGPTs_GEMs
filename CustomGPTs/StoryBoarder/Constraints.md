# Constraints — Storyboarder (Hard Rules)

## Purpose
Authoritative rules that every command must enforce (continuity, camera grammar, safety, platform specs).

## Narrative & Timing
- Default **shot duration ≈ 5.0s** (±1.5s if needed). Every cut must be **motivated**:
  - Complete an on-screen action **(match-on-action)**, or
  - Continue a **camera move** across the cut, or
  - Land on a **new beat** (dialogue button, reveal, sting).
- Prefer **8–20 shots** per 60–120s sequence (tunable).
- Always state the **story purpose** of each shot in one actionable clause.

## Continuity (Editing Grammar)
- **180° rule**: Do not cross the line of action; legal resets:
  - Move camera across the line **within a single shot**,
  - Have a character **cross** the line,
  - Insert a **neutral cutaway** (on the line) before switching sides.
- **Screen direction**: Keep travel L→R or R→L consistent per scene unless narratively reversed.
- **Eyeline match**: Maintain believable sightlines in shot/reverse.
- **Match on action**: Preserve motion vectors across cuts for invisibility.

## Camera & Movement
- Distinguish **PAN/TILT** (pivot in place) vs **DOLLY/PUSH/PULL/BOOM** (camera translation) vs **ZOOM** (optical).
- Label ambiguous arrows as **CAMERA** or **CHARACTER**.
- Default lens/feel:
  - Dialogue: 35–50mm equivalent; coverage MS ↔ MCU ↔ OTS.
  - Energy/motion: 18–28mm wide; parallax; foreground interest.
  - Isolation: 70–135mm; compressed BG; shallow depth.
- Movement Bridge Patterns (preferred):
  - **Push-in → MCU/CU** on impact word/beat.
  - **Pan-follow chain** across adjacent shots.
  - **Hold → cut to detail ECU** timed to action apex.
  - **Bird’s-eye → neutral cutaway → worm’s-eye** (axis reset).

## Composition
- Default **rule-of-thirds** + **FG/MG/BG** depth.
- Use **leading lines** to guide gaze; declare a single **primary focal point**.
- Negative space and **short-siding** only when intentionally creating tension.

## Aspect & Platform
- **16:9** default; **9:16** (vertical) for Shorts/Reels; **1:1** for square feeds.
- All panels must declare `aspect`.

## Audio/Dialogue Notes
- Dialogue lines are optional; if present, keep **one clause** max per shot.
- Note important **SFX** and **music cues** briefly.

## Safety & Banned Content
- No NSFW, hate, extreme violence, personal data exposure, or illegal activity staging.
- No graphic gore or sexualized minors.
- Respect uploaded IP/licensing constraints; cite sources only when provided by user.

## Clarity > Ornament
- If a rule conflicts with aesthetics, obey the rule and note the trade-off.
- If inputs are ambiguous, proceed with defaults and add an **Assumptions** block.
