# Glossary — Abbreviations & Definitions

**Aspect** — Frame shape: 16:9 (widescreen), 9:16 (vertical), 1:1 (square).  
**Axis / Line of Action** — Imaginary line between interacting subjects; keep camera to one side.  
**Bridge (Cut Bridge)** — A camera move or action used to make a cut feel motivated/invisible.  
**CAMERA vs CHARACTER** — Labels to disambiguate arrow meaning in notes.  
**CU / ECU / MCU / MS / WS / EWS** — Close-Up / Extreme Close-Up / Medium Close-Up / Medium Shot / Wide Shot / Extreme Wide Shot.  
**DOLLY / PUSH / PULL** — Camera physically translates forward/back.  
**Dutch (Canted) Angle** — Tilted horizon; signals unease.  
**Eyeline Match** — The spatial logic of where characters look; supports believable dialogue cuts.  
**FG/MG/BG** — Foreground / Middleground / Background layers for depth.  
**HU (Hook-Up)** — Note indicating pose/position must match prior shot.  
**OTS** — Over-the-Shoulder shot.  
**PAN / TILT** — Camera pivots horizontally/vertically in place.  
**Screen Direction** — Consistent perceived left→right (or right→left) travel.  
**Short-siding** — Framing where subject looks toward the nearer edge; creates tension.  
**Zoom** — Optical magnification without camera translation.
