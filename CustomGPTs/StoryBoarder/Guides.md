# Guides — Shot Sizes, Angles, Composition, Match-on-Action

## Shot Sizes (Abbrev → Purpose)
- **EWS/ELS**: world > subject; scale, loneliness, context.
- **WS/LS**: full body in environment; geography.
- **FS**: head-to-toe emphasis; physical action, stance.
- **MWS/CS**: knees/midthigh-up; group blocking, readiness.
- **MS**: waist-up; default dialog coverage.
- **MCU**: chest/shoulders-up; emotional focus.
- **CU**: face/detail; intimacy/emphasis.
- **ECU**: tiny detail (eyes, trigger); intensity/symbol.

## Angles (Height → Psychology)
- **Low**: power/heroic/menace.
- **High**: vulnerability/weakness.
- **Eye-level**: neutral/relatable.
- **Worm’s-eye**: awe/monumental.
- **Bird’s-eye**: omniscient/patterns.
- **Dutch (canted)**: unease/disorientation (use sparingly).

## Composition
- **Rule-of-thirds** by default.
- Declare a single **primary focal point** per frame.
- Build **FG/MG/BG** depth; even in CU use FG hints for depth.
- Use **leading lines**; avoid competing highlights near edges.
- **Short-siding** creates tension intentionally.

## Motion Notation (Arrows + Labels)
- **PAN L/R**, **TILT U/D** (pivot).
- **DOLLY/PUSH/PULL/BOOM** (translate).
- **ZOOM IN/OUT** (optical).
- Combine moves (e.g., **TILT+PAN**); label ambiguous arrows **CAMERA** or **CHARACTER**.
- Continuity marks: **IN** (enter), **OUT** (exit), **HU** (hook-up pose).

## Continuity & Match-on-Action (Primer)
- Keep **line of action** consistent (180° rule).
- Preserve **screen direction** for moving subjects.
- **Match-on-action**: start motion in shot A, cut mid-motion, complete in shot B.
- Legal axis crossing: in-shot camera cross, character cross, or neutral cutaway.

## Cut Concealment for 5s Generators
- Make each 5s block **complete an action** or **continue a move**.
- End on a **button** (beat, word, gesture) to motivate the cut.
- Bridge patterns: push-in → CU; pan-follow chain; hold → ECU detail.
