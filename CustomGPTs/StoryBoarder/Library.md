# Library — Reference Sequences & Move Bridges

## Sequence A: “Fence Walk” (16:9, Warm Indie 35mm)
Goal: Introduce character; set travel direction and tone.

1) **S01-SH01** — WS, eye-level, 24mm, **PAN RIGHT**  
   Action: Protagonist walks along fence; wind lifts jacket hem.  
   Bridge: Continue pan into SH02 to hide cut.

2) **S01-SH02** — MS, eye-level, 35mm, **DOLLY IN**  
   Action: Hand pulls photo from pocket, glance to corner.  
   Bridge: Cut on the **hand motion** into SH03 (match-on-action).

3) **S01-SH03** — MCU, low, 50mm, **HOLD**  
   Action: Small smile forms; eyes glisten.  
   Bridge: Land smile on lyric beat → cut to ECU detail.

4) **S01-SH04** — ECU, eye-level, 85mm, **HOLD**  
   Action: Photo corner; thumb smoothing crease.  
   Bridge: Neutral cutaway available for axis reset if needed.

## Sequence B: “Crossing the Line (Legal Reset)”
- SH01: OTS-A→B (MS, 35mm), dialogue cue.
- SH02: **Neutral insert** of object between characters (CU).
- SH03: OTS-B→A (MS, 35mm), now on opposite side legally.

## Move Bridge Recipes
- **Pan-chain**: PAN L/R across 2–3 shots; anchor on a moving subject.
- **Push-to-button**: PUSH IN → cut to MCU/CU on keyword or reveal.
- **Hold→Detail**: Static wide → cut to ECU on action apex.
- **Overhead reset**: Bird’s-eye insert before switching to low angle (axis reset).
