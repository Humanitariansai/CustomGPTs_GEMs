# Look — Reference Styles & Prompt Macros

## Macro Variables
- `{style}` — short style tag list (e.g., "warm indie 35mm, soft grain, natural light")
- `{srefs}` — style reference IDs or links (e.g., "sref:lookbook/indie_daylight")
- `{aspect}` — 16:9 | 9:16 | 1:1
- `{pcodes}` — personalization/style codes if available

## Style Cards

### Warm Indie 35mm (LC-WI35)
- Palette: warm skins, teal shadows, low saturation.
- Lighting: natural, soft edge.
- Texture: light grain, gentle halation.

### Epic Wide 18mm (LC-EW18)
- Palette: vibrant skies, high micro-contrast.
- Lighting: backlight edges; silhouette-friendly.
- Texture: crisp, minimal grain.

### Noir Telephoto (LC-NT85)
- Palette: cool shadows, silver highlights.
- Lighting: motivated practicals, pools of light.
- Texture: medium grain, high contrast.
