# Styles — Look Packs, Lenses, Palettes, Aspect Guides

## Selection Policy
- Default pick: 70% from **Preferred Packs**, 30% from **Alternates**.
- Packs define **lens tendencies**, **palette**, **grain/contrast**, and **aspect** defaults.

## Preferred Packs
### 1) Warm Indie 35mm
- Lens: 35–50mm normals; occasional 24mm for establishing.
- Palette: warm skin tones, teal-shadows, natural light.
- Grain/Contrast: light grain, soft contrast.
- Aspect: 16:9 (default).
- Use when: intimate character beats; walk-and-talk; handheld feel.

### 2) Epic Wide 18mm
- Lens: 18–24mm; parallax foregrounds.
- Palette: high-key skies, lifted mids; subtle vignettes.
- Grain/Contrast: clean, crisp edges.
- Aspect: 16:9 or 2.00:1.
- Use when: scale, travel, choreography, environmental storytelling.

### 3) Noir Telephoto
- Lens: 85–135mm; shallow depth; compressed backgrounds.
- Palette: low-key, cool shadows, specular highlights.
- Grain/Contrast: medium grain, high contrast.
- Aspect: 16:9.
- Use when: isolation, surveillance, tension.

## Alternates
### 4) Pastel Diary
- Lens: 28–40mm; soft edges.
- Palette: pastel hues, low saturation.
- Aspect: 1:1 or 9:16.
- Mood: nostalgic, tender.

### 5) Neon Grit
- Lens: 24–35mm; mixed color temps.
- Palette: magenta/cyan neons, deep blacks.
- Aspect: 16:9.
- Mood: urban velocity, night energy.

## Aspect Guide
- YouTube/desktop: **16:9**
- Reels/Shorts/TikTok: **9:16**
- Instagram grid: **1:1**
- For multi-platform delivery, compose with **safe center**; ensure critical action stays in the middle third.

## Lens Intent Cheats
- **Wide (18–28mm)**: speed, parallax, spatial exaggeration; add foreground.
- **Normal (35–50mm)**: neutral perspective; dialogue, medium coverage.
- **Tele (70–135mm)**: isolation, compression, portrait emotion.
